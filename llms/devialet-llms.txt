## About

**Devialet** is a French company specializing in cutting-edge audio technologies. Founded and based in **Paris**, Devialet designs and develops high-end acoustic systems that combine **refined design, precision engineering, and exceptional sound performance**.

The company is behind several **patented innovations** in the audio field, including **ADH® (Analog Digital Hybrid)**, **SAM® (Speaker Active Matching)**, and real-time **audio signal processing technologies**.

Devialet represents French technological excellence in the audio world, with an international presence and a reputation for innovation.

## Key Technologies

- **ADH® (Analog Digital Hybrid)**: Combines the accuracy of analog amplification with the power of digital.
- **SAM® (Speaker Active Matching)**: Real-time audio signal optimization tailored to each speaker's specifications.
- **EVO® Platform**: Embedded scalable system supporting software updates and continuous improvements.
- **ANC Devialet™**: Advanced active noise cancellation featured in Gemini earbuds.
